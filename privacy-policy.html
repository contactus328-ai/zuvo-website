ZUVO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zhev Education handles information when you use ZUVO and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your use of ZUVO, information may include your name, email address, mobile number, college or institution, information you choose to provide, event registrations, device and technical information, and information about your use of the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Provide event discovery and registration services.
  • Send event and service-related communications.
  • Connect users with relevant institutions and event organisers.
  • Maintain security and improve the service.
  • Comply with applicable law.

3. Sharing

When you register for an event, information needed to administer that event may be shared with the relevant organiser, college or institution.

4. Third-Party Services

ZUVO may use service providers for hosting, authentication, notifications, analytics, security and other technical functions. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

5. Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures designed to protect personal information and retain information only as reasonably necessary for service, legal, security and legitimate business purposes.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ZUVO account and associated personal information, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
