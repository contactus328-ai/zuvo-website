ABOUT ZUVO

Campus life, easier to discover.

ZUVO is an event discovery and engagement platform designed to help students discover, explore and register for college, university and related events.

What ZUVO does

ZUVO brings event information into one student-friendly experience. Students can discover opportunities, understand event details and register where registration is available.

Designed for students and institutions

The platform is intended to connect students with participating colleges, universities and event organisers while giving organisers a clearer way to present their events to an interested audience.
